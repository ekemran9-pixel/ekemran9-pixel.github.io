Privacy Policy

FabCalc Pro — Fabrication Calculator · Effective 6 November 2026

FabCalc Pro is a calculator app developed by Emran-mia. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device only

Your settings (language, units, theme), favourites, calculation history and your own pipe-colour table are saved in your phone’s browser storage so the app works offline. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it any time in Settings → Clear saved data, or by clearing the app’s storage / uninstalling.

Motion sensor

The Spirit Level tool reads the phone’s motion / orientation sensor only while that screen is open, to show tilt angles. The readings are processed on the device and are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Sharing and printing

When you tap Share, Print or Save, the report image or text is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or a printer). FabCalc Pro does not receive a copy.

Network

The app is a web app hosted on GitHub Pages. When online it downloads its own program files from ekemran9-pixel.github.io to stay up to date; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. No calculation data is sent.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
